About

A barber who comes to you

Royal Barber is run by Nigel, an expert barber working across Bulawayo. You will find him in the CBD at Modern Classics Saloon, 106 Main Street, between 10 & 11 Avenue — along JM Nkomo Street, opposite Toyota — and at Styles Saloon No. 7491, Bullet Shopping Centre, Nkulumane 5.

If getting to a salon is difficult, the barber comes to you. House calls cover the same careful work, and wedding bookings are available for a whole team of up to 10 people. Send your location on WhatsApp and we will take it from there.

Doors are open from 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and appointments continue even during ZESA load shedding.

  • Precision cuts, ladies cuts, colour enhancements and beard work.
  • Two salons in Bulawayo, plus house calls across the city.
  • Open through power cuts — your appointment still happens.
